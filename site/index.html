Zero-Knowledge Sync Principle: The daemon binds exclusively to 127.0.0.1. External telemetry is physically impossible because the daemon does not compile or include any outbound HTTP analytics client. History and cookies are stored unencrypted only inside your local device's isolated SQLite database.
